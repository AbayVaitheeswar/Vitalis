How it works

Three simple steps. No apps to install, no forms to fill out.

1

A doctor opens a slot

A volunteer doctor picks a date and time whenever they have 15 minutes free.

2

You pick a time

Browse the open times and book the one that suits you. That's your appointment.

3

You have your call

At the right time, tap "Join call" — a private video call opens in your browser.
